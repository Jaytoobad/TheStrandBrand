Our story

We believe good hair shouldn't come with a guessing game.

TheStrandBrand started with a simple frustration: it was too hard to find wigs and bundles that actually matched what the photos promised. So we built the store we wished existed — every bundle single-donor, every wig hand-tied, every price honest.

What we're about

We're a small team obsessed with texture, longevity, and making sure a customer three time zones away trusts what lands on their doorstep.

Our core values

Honesty in every strand

What you see is what you get — no bleached photos, no vague listings. If a bundle is limited or sold out, we say so.

Quality over volume

We'd rather stock fewer, better bundles than flood the shelf with anything that wears thin after a month.

Real people, real support

Every order is tracked and every question gets answered by someone who actually knows the stock.

Built to last

Our bundles and wigs are chosen for how they hold up wash after wash, not just how they photograph.

What we're aiming for

To become the store people trust first when they think about hair — not because we're the biggest, but because we never let a customer down about what's actually in the box.
