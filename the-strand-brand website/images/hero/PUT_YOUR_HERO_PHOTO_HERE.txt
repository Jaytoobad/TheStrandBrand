Put your homepage background photo here, named exactly:

    hero.jpg

That's the only step — index.html is already set up to look for
images/hero/hero.jpg. A wide, landscape photo (at least 1600px wide)
works best, since it stretches across the full width of the screen
behind the "Hair that holds its own." headline.

If you'd rather keep a different file name, open index.html, find the
<section class="hero" ...> line near the top, and change the
background-image url() to match your file name.
